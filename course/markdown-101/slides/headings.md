## Headings

```markdown
# Project Phoenix

## Goals

### Stretch goals

Launch the beta by June.
```

- Use `#` for the level 1 heading, `##` for level 2, and `###` for level 3
- A space after the `#`, and a blank line before and after
- Only one `# Title` per file, and don't skip levels
